LEARN THE LOGICHow it works+

01 · LIFO

A stack follows Last In, First Out. The most recently pushed item is the first one removed. This makes stacks useful for operators and operands.

02 · Precedence

Exponentiation has the highest precedence, then multiplication and division, then addition and subtraction. The exponent operator is right-associative.

03 · Three notations

Infix places operators between operands (3 + 4). Postfix puts them after (3 4 +). Prefix puts them before (+ 3 4).

04 · Complexity

For n tokens, parsing, conversion and evaluation take O(n) time and O(n) auxiliary space, excluding the cost of very large-number arithmetic.

Grammar note: Operands are unsigned integer or decimal numbers. Unary plus/minus are intentionally rejected. Infix supports () and []; prefix and postfix inputs are whitespace-separated tokens.
